# Motion reference — Benji Taylor, "Grok Bot templates" (x.com, Aug 28 2026)

16.5s, 1920×1080, six shots, hard cuts. What the film should borrow.

## The camera never rests
Every shot has a slow push or pan. The UI is oversized and cropped by the
frame — the window is a set, not a screenshot — and the push accelerates
toward whatever the cursor is about to touch (the share button, the card's
CTA). A shot opens at fit scale and ends 1.5–2.5× in.

## Hard cuts, one ease
Shots cut; nothing crossfades. Inside a shot everything shares one
deceleration (an ease-out, cubic-ish). Panels slide in over the pane they
cover and push it aside (the settings panel enters from the right, the
chat slides left about half its width). Buttons darken on hover, press on
click; a CTA fills from grey to ink in ~0.4s.

## Type cards
Between app shots: cut to flat white, the line arrives word by word from
the right (~150ms cadence, each word decelerating into place, the line
re-centring as it grows), then holds ~1s. Nothing else on the card.

## Grounds
Bright blue grain gradient for the app; flat white for type; light grey for
the share card. Three grounds, each owning a shot type.

## Cursor
Big, physical, and the reason the camera moves. Every app shot is a hand
going to a control: approach, hover state, press, result.

## Ending
White. The mark drops in with an overshoot (size bounce with a highlight),
then slides left as the wordmark lands beside it. Hold.
